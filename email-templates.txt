COMBOAPP EMAIL SETUP
====================

1) Supabase Dashboard > Authentication > Email Templates > Reset Password
2) Replace the template body with the HTML below.
3) Do the same for Confirm Signup, changing the button URL to {{ .ConfirmationURL }}.
4) Host logo.png on your HTTPS app domain and replace LOGO_URL.

CUSTOM SENDER
-------------
The visible sender name "ComboApp" is controlled by your SMTP provider, not JavaScript.
In Supabase: Authentication > SMTP Settings, enter your provider SMTP host/user/password and set the sender name/email to ComboApp.
Never put SMTP passwords or API secrets inside app.js.
